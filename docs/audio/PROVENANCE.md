# Provenance and use notes

All audio in this delivery was composed/sequenced and synthesized for Echo Heist
during the earlier audio-production task. No third-party music recordings, samples, soundfonts, voice
recordings, commercial tracks or copyrighted melody prompts were incorporated.
The timbres are mathematical oscillators, filtered generated noise and original
percussive recipes. This slim handoff includes the rendered audio, metadata, and integration notes; the synthesis source and score remain in the separately delivered original audio pack.

This is a provenance statement, not a guarantee of exclusive copyright or a
third-party legal opinion. The pack does not require attribution to an external
asset creator because no external asset library was used. Use of generated
outputs remains subject to your applicable service terms and local law; this
document does not attempt to assign rights the generator does not hold.

Software dependencies are NOT copied into this pack. Their own licenses apply
to their installation/use. FFmpeg was used only as an encoder. No music-generation
model weights or paid generation APIs were used. The runtime audio player needs
only browser APIs and locally hosted files; No audio regeneration is required for this handoff.

Audio status: delivered synthesized first-production-pass assets. Human approval
of the soundtrack, repetition fatigue, mixing on actual student hardware, and
final in-game balance remain open. Do not relabel this as recorded Foley or a
studio-recorded orchestral score.
