# Music and atmosphere guide

The same theme serves the five missions of its chapter. Adaptive stems add tension without restarting.

## Arrangement

Each chapter has a complete base score, an additive alert layer and an additive extraction layer. The three WAV masters have identical lengths and shared time origin. Their balance was scaled as a group, not normalized independently. Each 24-bar chapter loop has three eight-bar phrases. Screen cues have 16 bars, except the 24-bar credits cue.

Scores and electronic robot phrases are original procedural compositions. Environmental ambience is an independent low-level loop. No compulsory dialogue or educational narration is present.
